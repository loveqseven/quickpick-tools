# Launch checklist

1. Replace https://example.com in canonical tags, sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your real HTTPS domain.
2. Replace placeholder contact/privacy/terms information with your actual business details and services.
3. Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.
4. Test all tool pages, forms, mobile layout and internal links.
5. Apply for AdSense only after the site has substantial original, useful content.
6. Replace ADVERTISEMENT placeholders with approved ad units. Keep ads clearly separated from controls and never encourage clicks.
7. FAQPage JSON-LD is included only for visible FAQs; keep structured data synchronized with page content.
8. Continue adding original examples, use cases and related-tool content to priority pages.
